BB84, photon by photon

Alice sends photons, each carrying one bit in one of two bases, + or x. Bob measures each in a basis of his own choosing. Where the bases match he reads her bit; where they differ he gets a coin flip. Anyone who listens has to measure too, and that leaves errors behind. The photons here are simulated.
